What it's like to live in Miami Beach

From South Beach's Art Deco district to quiet Mid-Beach streets and private islands, all on the ocean.

Get Miami Beach homes that match WhatsApp me about Miami Beach

Miami Beach is its own city on a barrier island across Biscayne Bay. It includes South Beach and its Art Deco Historic District, quieter Mid-Beach and North Beach, and residential islands like the Venetian Islands and Star Island.

Buyers come for oceanfront condos, waterfront homes and the beach lifestyle, from second-home buyers to families who live here year-round.

What you can buy

  • Oceanfront and bayfront condos, from classic to new luxury towers
  • Waterfront single-family homes and island estates
  • Boutique Art Deco buildings

Who it's great for

  • Second-home and international buyers who want the beach
  • Families looking for waterfront homes
  • Buyers who want walkable beach neighborhoods

What to check before you buy

  • Short-term rental rules: Miami Beach restricts them in many areas
  • Flood zone, elevation and insurance
  • Condo reserves and inspection status in older buildings

FAQ

Miami Beach questions buyers ask

Can I rent my Miami Beach property on Airbnb?

Miami Beach restricts short-term rentals in many residential areas, and many condo buildings ban them. Check zoning and building rules before buying for rental income.

What's the difference between South Beach and Mid-Beach?

South Beach is busier, with the Art Deco district and nightlife; Mid-Beach and North Beach are generally quieter and more residential.

Is flood insurance required on Miami Beach?

It depends on the property's flood zone and your lender. Ask for the elevation certificate and an insurance quote before you make an offer.
