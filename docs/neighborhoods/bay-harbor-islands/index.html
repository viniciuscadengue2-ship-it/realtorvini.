What it's like to live in Bay Harbor Islands

Low-rise condos, single-family homes and a quiet, walkable town.

Get Bay Harbor Islands homes that match WhatsApp me about Bay Harbor Islands

Bay Harbor Islands is a small town made up of two islands in Biscayne Bay, next to Bal Harbour. The east island has low-rise condos and a walkable business district; the west island is mostly single-family homes.

It's popular with buyers who want a quiet, walkable community close to the beach and Bal Harbour Shops.

What you can buy

  • Low-rise and boutique condos
  • Single-family homes, some on the water
  • New boutique buildings

Who it's great for

  • Buyers who want walkability and a small-town feel
  • Families looking for homes near the beach
  • Condo buyers who prefer low-rise buildings

What to check before you buy

  • Condo reserves, milestone inspection and planned assessments
  • Flood zone, elevation certificate and insurance quote
  • Age of older low-rise buildings

FAQ

Bay Harbor Islands questions buyers ask

Where is Bay Harbor Islands?

It's two islands in Biscayne Bay, just west of Bal Harbour and Surfside, connected by the Broad Causeway to the mainland.

What homes are in Bay Harbor Islands?

Low-rise condos on the east island and mostly single-family homes on the west island.
