What it's like to live in Brickell

High-rise living, walkable streets and the city's busiest restaurant and office scene, just south of the Miami River.

Get Brickell homes that match WhatsApp me about Brickell

Brickell is Miami's financial district and one of its most walkable neighborhoods. Glass condo towers line Brickell Avenue and Biscayne Bay, with Brickell City Centre, Mary Brickell Village and dozens of restaurants within a few blocks.

The free Metromover connects Brickell to Downtown, and Key Biscayne and the beaches are a short drive away. It's popular with professionals, international buyers and investors who want a lock-and-leave home.

What you can buy

  • High-rise condos, from studios to bayfront penthouses
  • Newer luxury towers with full amenities
  • A smaller number of townhomes and single-family homes in South Brickell

Who it's great for

  • Professionals who want to walk to work and restaurants
  • International buyers looking for a lock-and-leave home
  • Investors comparing rental-friendly buildings

What to check before you buy

  • Each building's rental rules (minimum lease terms vary a lot)
  • HOA fees, reserves and any planned special assessments
  • Milestone inspection and reserve study (SIRS) status
  • Whether the building is approved for conventional financing

FAQ

Brickell questions buyers ask

Is Brickell a good place to invest in a condo?

It can be, but returns depend on the building more than the neighborhood: rental rules, HOA fees, reserves and insurance. I compare buildings side by side before you make an offer.

Can I rent my Brickell condo on Airbnb?

Only in buildings whose rules allow short-term rentals, and many don't. Always confirm the condo documents and city rules before buying for short-term rental.

Is Brickell walkable?

Yes. It's one of Miami's most walkable areas, with shops, restaurants, offices and the free Metromover close together.
