What it's like to live in Hialeah

Affordable homes, strong community and central access to the airport and highways.

Get Hialeah homes that match WhatsApp me about Hialeah

Hialeah is one of the largest cities in Miami-Dade, with a strong Cuban-American community, local businesses and a lively, close-knit feel.

It offers some of the more affordable single-family homes and condos in the county, with quick access to the airport, Doral and the highways.

What you can buy

  • Single-family homes
  • Condos and townhomes
  • Small multifamily properties

Who it's great for

  • First-time buyers looking for value
  • Investors interested in rentals
  • Families with ties to the community

What to check before you buy

  • Permits for past additions and converted spaces
  • Roof age and insurance
  • Rental rules and current leases on income properties

FAQ

Hialeah questions buyers ask

Is Hialeah affordable?

It has some of the more affordable homes in Miami-Dade compared with coastal neighborhoods.

What should investors check in Hialeah?

Permits for any converted spaces, current leases and the property's condition.
