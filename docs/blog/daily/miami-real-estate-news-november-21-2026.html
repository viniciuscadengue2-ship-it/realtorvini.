Miami Real Estate News, Nov. 21, 2026: Daily Mortgage Rates Ease to 7.33% as Pending Home Sales Fall 16.7% and the Dollar Tops R$5.90

I also look at two large towers proposed for Brickell and Edgewater. Under each story is what I'm telling my own clients.

Mortgage Rates

1. Daily 30-year rates slip to 7.33% after Thursday's weekly increase

Mortgage News Daily's index of 30-year fixed rates fell to 7.33% on November 20, down 0.12 point from 7.45% the day before and its lowest reading in the past week. A year earlier the same index stood at 6.36%. Mortgage Research Center data for November 19 put the average 30-year fixed purchase rate at 7.29% and the 15-year at 6.46%, with 30-year jumbo loans at 7.47%.

These daily readings sit just above Freddie Mac's weekly average, which rose to 7.28% on November 19 from 7.11%. Freddie Mac publishes once a week, so Friday's dip would show up in next Thursday's survey only if it holds.

My take: On a $500,000 30-year loan, principal and interest is about $3,438 a month at 7.33%, roughly $41 less than at 7.45% ($3,479), and about $324 more than at a year-ago 6.36% ($3,114). Day-to-day moves like this are small, so don't try to time the bottom. If you're under contract, ask your lender for a written quote today and decide on a lock with real numbers. If you're shopping for a jumbo loan, common for Miami houses, compare at least three lenders, because jumbo pricing varies more than the averages suggest.

Source: Mortgage News Daily, 30-year fixed index · Mortgage Research Center, Nov. 19 · Fortune, Nov. 19

U.S. Market

2. Contract signings fell 16.7% from a year ago in October, with the South down 18.7%

The National Association of Realtors' Pending Home Sales Index fell 4.7% in October from September, to 63.0, and was down 16.7% from a year earlier, NAR reported on November 18. In the South, which includes Florida, the index fell 6.3% for the month to 74.5 and 18.7% from a year earlier. NAR's chief economist, Lawrence Yun, said October "could mark the low point in contract signings" for this cycle.

Closings tell a similar story. NAR's November 12 report put October existing-home sales at a seasonally adjusted annual pace of 3.75 million, down 5.5% from September and 8.8% from a year earlier, the lowest level since August 2010. The national median price was $416,700, up 0.4% from a year earlier, and supply rose to 5.1 months from 4.4 months a year ago.

My take: Fewer contracts in October means fewer closings in November and December, here and nationally. Prices are holding nearly flat rather than falling, which matches what we see in Miami-Dade houses. For buyers, a slow holiday season is a good time to negotiate, especially on condos. For sellers, the homes that sell now are the ones priced right and ready to show. If you want to know how your building or street is doing, send me a WhatsApp and I'll pull the recent sales.

Source: NAR, October pending home sales · NAR, October existing-home sales

Brazilian Buyers

3. The dollar climbs past R$5.90 as Brazil's Congress debates a tighter spending cap

The U.S. dollar peaked at R$5.914 on Thursday, November 19, after a fifth straight daily gain on Wednesday, the Rio Times reported, calling it a 19-month high for the dollar. Trading Economics reported the real had already slipped to around 5.80 per dollar on November 16 and passed 5.90 after the Senate vote.

The move followed the Senate's approval of a bill, PLP 299/2026, that lowers the cap on real government spending growth from 2.5% to 2% starting in 2027, according to the Rio Times. The lower house is expected to vote on Monday, November 23. Trading Economics also noted that Brazil's October inflation, 4.63%, was above the central bank's 4.5% ceiling.

My take: For a Brazilian buyer, the exchange rate is part of the price. At R$5.914 per dollar, a US$500,000 condo costs about R$2.96 million, versus R$2.9 million at 5.80 just a few days earlier. I can't tell you where the real will go next, and neither can anyone else. What I tell my clients is to plan the transfer in stages, ask your bank or a licensed exchange service about locking a rate for your deposit dates, and talk to a CPA or attorney who works in both countries before you sign. New Development

4. An 834-foot Brickell tower and a 43-story Edgewater building head to design review

Empira Group and Partners Group have filed plans for B Residences Miami, an 834-foot tower at 11 SW 7th Street in Brickell with 369 residences from about 660 to 2,765 square feet, plus two 5,574-square-foot penthouses, Florida YIMBY reported on November 15. The Arquitectonica design also includes 14,758 square feet of retail and 707 parking spaces. An earlier announcement targeted a construction start in late 2028 and completion in 2031.

In Edgewater, Cain and Kushner have proposed a 43-story, 527-foot waterfront tower at 720 NE 27th Street with 392 residences, also designed by Arquitectonica, Florida YIMBY reported on November 16. Both projects were scheduled to go before Miami's Urban Development Review Board on November 18. Nearby, Bisnow reported on November 16 that Two Roads is demolishing the Biscayne 21 building at 2121 N. Bayshore Drive to make way for the 55-story, 185-unit EDITION Residences Edgewater.

My take: Developers keep betting on Brickell and Edgewater even with a lot of resale condos on the market, which tells you where they expect long-term demand. If you own a resale condo in these areas, expect more competition from new product later this decade, so keep your building's reserves, inspections and insurance in good order; that is what buyers compare. If preconstruction interests you, remember that a design review is an early step. I wait for approvals, financing and a groundbreaking before treating a tower as real, and I always read the deposit schedule first.

Source: Florida YIMBY, B Residences Miami · Florida YIMBY, 720 NE 27th Street · Bisnow South Florida Deal Sheet

Miami real estate questions people are asking

What are mortgage rates today in Miami?

Mortgage News Daily's index put the average 30-year fixed rate at 7.33% on November 20, 2026, and Freddie Mac's weekly average was 7.28% on November 19. Miami buyers get rates close to national averages; your rate depends on credit, down payment and loan type.

Are home sales slowing in the U.S.?

Yes. NAR's Pending Home Sales Index fell 16.7% in October 2026 from a year earlier, and 18.7% in the South. Existing-home sales ran at a 3.75 million annual pace, the lowest since August 2010, while the median price rose 0.4% to $416,700.

How does the dollar-real exchange rate affect Brazilians buying in Miami?

Miami homes are priced in dollars, so a weaker real raises the cost in reais. At R$5.914 per dollar (the November 19 peak reported by the Rio Times), a US$500,000 property costs about R$2.96 million. Plan transfers with your bank and a CPA.

What new condo towers are planned in Brickell?

One of the latest is B Residences Miami, an 834-foot tower with 369 residences proposed at 11 SW 7th Street by Empira Group and Partners Group, which was scheduled for Miami design review on November 18, 2026, according to Florida YIMBY.

Bottom line

Daily rates eased to 7.33% at the end of the week, but national contract signings are down sharply from last year, so expect a slow, negotiable holiday market. Brazilian buyers should plan their currency transfers carefully with the dollar above R$5.90. And developers are still lining up big towers in Brickell and Edgewater.
