Insurance

1. Most Citizens policyholders with wind coverage need a flood policy by January 1, 2027

Citizens Property Insurance has been phasing in a rule that requires policyholders with wind coverage to also carry flood insurance, whether or not the home sits in a FEMA flood zone. Homes valued at $600,000 and up had to comply by January 1, 2024, $500,000 and up by 2025 and $400,000 and up by 2026. All remaining homes follow on January 1, 2027, News4Jax reported. A Florida brokerage summary of the rule says the flood policy must cover at least the dwelling amount or the federal maximum if that is lower, that proof is required, and that condo unit-owner policies, renters policies and Citizens policies without wind coverage are exempt.

Separately, Citizens keeps moving policies to private insurers through its depopulation program. CubaHeadlines reported that homeowners who received offer letters on October 28 have until December 4 to choose, with transfers scheduled for December 15, and that Citizens' policy count fell from 1.4 million in 2023 to 266,231 by August 2026.

My take: If you own a house insured with Citizens, call your insurance agent this month, not in late December. Ask how long a new flood policy takes to start, so you don't end up with a gap in coverage over the holidays. If you're buying a home that's currently insured with Citizens, get a flood quote during your inspection period and add it to your monthly budget. Condo owners: your own unit policy is exempt, but ask the association how the building itself is insured.

Source: News4Jax · Jared Jones Real Estate Team · CubaHeadlines

Mortgage Rates

2. Thursday's dip didn't last: daily 30-year rates are back between 7.26% and 7.42%

Freddie Mac's weekly average fell to 7.11% on Thursday, but daily trackers had already moved higher. Mortgage Research Center data reviewed by Fortune put the average 30-year conventional rate at 7.263% for November 12, up from 7.046% a week earlier, and the 15-year at 6.439%. Mortgage News Daily's index closed November 12 at 7.42%, up 0.12 point in a day.

Buyers are still showing up. Fortune noted the Mortgage Bankers Association counted a 6.2% rise in applications for the week ending November 6. The Federal Reserve's last meeting of the year is December 8-9.

My take: On a $500,000 30-year loan, principal and interest is about $3,364 a month at 7.11%, $3,415 at 7.263% and $3,469 at 7.42%. The weekly Freddie Mac number is an average of the past week, so it often trails what lenders quote today. If you're under contract, ask your lender for a same-day written quote and decide on a lock with real numbers in front of you.

Source: Fortune (Mortgage Research Center data) · Mortgage News Daily

Edgewater

3. Demolition begins at Biscayne 21, clearing the way for the 649-foot EDITION Residences on the bay

Demolition has started at 2121 North Bayshore Drive in Edgewater, where Two Roads Development plans EDITION Residences, Miami Edgewater, a 55-story, 649-foot tower with 185 condos designed by Arquitectonica, Florida YIMBY reported. The 13-story Biscayne 21 building on the site, just north of Margaret Pace Park, had been tied up in litigation since 2023 over the condo's termination; Two Roads settled with the remaining owners in September, and a total-demolition permit was issued August 26.

Residences range from about 1,922 to 3,760 square feet, with three tri-level penthouses, on more than 800 feet of bay frontage. A brokerage inventory guide cited by Haute Residence lists starting prices from $3.715 million.

My take: Biscayne 21 is the clearest local example of a condo termination: older buildings facing big repair bills, with land worth more than the units, are getting bought out and replaced. If you own in an older bayfront building, it's worth knowing what your land is worth and how your association's termination rules work. If you're buying preconstruction, a demolition permit is real progress, but the milestone that matters most is the start of vertical construction.

Source: Florida YIMBY · Haute Residence

Wealth Migration

4. California's top agents follow their clients to Miami, and Ken Griffin is nominated for a $2 million club membership

The Altman Brothers, the Los Angeles luxury team led by Josh and Matt Altman and Heather Altman, are opening a South Florida office through Douglas Elliman and bought a penthouse in Brickell, The Real Deal reported. The article cites Miami Realtors data showing South Florida driver license exchanges rose 16% in the first half of the year, with California the top state of origin. The team says it refers more than $100 million in business to the region each year.

In Coral Gables, Citadel founder Ken Griffin has been nominated to join Riviera Country Club under a new non-voting membership class offered to 10 people at $2 million, which lets members skip a wait list of more than 150 people, according to The Real Deal. The club's board is expected to vote later this month.

My take: When top agents from Beverly Hills open offices here, it's because their clients are already buying here. That steady flow of out-of-state money keeps supporting prices for well-located single-family homes and waterfront property. For my Brazilian and Latin American clients, it means competition for the best houses is coming from California and New York as much as from abroad, so being pre-approved or having proof of funds ready matters.

Source: The Real Deal (Altman Brothers) · The Real Deal (Riviera Country Club)

Miami real estate questions people are asking

Do Citizens policyholders in Florida need flood insurance?

Yes, most do. Citizens has phased in a requirement that policyholders with wind coverage also carry flood insurance, in any flood zone. The final phase, covering all remaining homes, takes effect January 1, 2027. Check your own policy with your insurance agent.

What are mortgage rates today in Miami?

Freddie Mac's weekly average for a 30-year fixed mortgage was 7.11% on November 12, 2026. Daily trackers were higher the same day: 7.263% (Mortgage Research Center) and 7.42% (Mortgage News Daily). Your rate depends on credit, down payment and loan type.

What is being built at Biscayne 21 in Edgewater?

Two Roads Development is building EDITION Residences, Miami Edgewater, a 55-story, 649-foot tower with 185 condos at 2121 North Bayshore Drive. Demolition of the old Biscayne 21 building began in November 2026.

Are people still moving to Miami from California?

Yes. Miami Realtors data cited by The Real Deal shows South Florida driver license exchanges rose 16% in the first half of 2026, with California the top state of origin.

Bottom line

If you insure a house with Citizens, put flood coverage on your to-do list now, because January 1 is close. Rates are back above 7.2% on daily trackers, so lock with real quotes, not headlines. And out-of-state wealth keeps arriving, which keeps the best Miami houses competitive.
